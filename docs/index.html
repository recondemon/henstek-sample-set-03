Local by default. Yours to carry.

All places and initial observations are fictional. Each app stores its own local data. Compatible JSON exports let you move a journal manually between them; there is no automatic sync or account. Imports replace the destination journal after confirmation. Keep exports for anything you want to retain. Files are readable, not encrypted.

The illustrated map is not for navigation. No location, weather or species identification service is connected. The iPhone screenshot is from the native Simulator. Native desktop verification covers Apple silicon macOS. Sources are available, with no store listing or signed installer.
